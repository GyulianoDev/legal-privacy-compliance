# Outline: Privacy Policy

Inventory-based draft; do not fill gaps with promises.

1. **Controller and scope:** responsible party, service, role per operation, version, contact and data protection officer only when confirmed.
2. **What is processed and why:** table per operation with necessary categories/fields, origin, data subjects, purpose and reasoned proposed basis; distinguish required and optional processing.
3. **Recipients:** provider/function, categories transmitted, role and conditions; do not state “we do not share” when hosting, AI or payments are involved.
4. **Transfers:** verified regions, remote access and confirmed legal mechanisms; unknown facts remain unresolved.
5. **Retention and deletion:** criteria per operation, accounts, logs, backups and preservation obligations; describe the actual process without promising immediate deletion.
6. **Security:** evidenced measures at an appropriate level, limitations and contact; no “100% secure” or unsupported end-to-end encryption claims.
7. **Rights and requests:** applicable rights, channel, proportionate authentication, procedure and legal restrictions; check current deadlines.
8. **Minors, cookies and AI:** include only when relevant, linking specific notices and reflecting actual data.
9. **Changes and contact:** date, version and the actual method used to communicate important changes.

Check each category/purpose against code, configuration, responsible-party confirmation and evidence. A detailed internal inventory may contain information that does not belong in the public version.
