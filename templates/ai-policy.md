# Outline: AI Policy

1. Features using AI, purpose, audience and the role of automated decisions.
2. Providers, API/plan/model when relevant; transmitted data, files, images/audio, prompts and responses.
3. History, memory, RAG, embeddings and vector databases; access, isolation, retention/deletion and third parties.
4. Verified provider training, retention and opt-out conditions. Without sufficient contracts/documentation, identify the gap instead of promising “never used for training.”
5. Agent tools/actions, permissions, limits and human-confirmation requirements in actual procedures.
6. Limitations, incorrect outputs, supervision, challenges and applicable rights; specific information about relevant decisions.
7. Security and acceptable use: data users should avoid submitting, third-party rights and incident handling.
8. Version, contact and relationship with the privacy policy/contract.

Separate effective requirements from recommendations. A “may be wrong” notice does not automatically transfer all liability to users.
