# Brazilian scope

Use the [source registry](source-registry.md) and research the text effective on the work date. This map guides research; it is neither a legal opinion nor a transcription of legislation.

| Rule | When to investigate | Provisions and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LGPD, Law 13.709/2018 | Processing within Article 3; check Article 4 exceptions | Articles 5–11, 14–20, 33–41, 46–50 depending on the operation. |
| Internet Civil Framework, Law 12.965/2014 | Internet applications and connections | Articles 7–11 and 13–15. Log retention obligations depend on provider type and legal conditions; do not impose six months on every website. |
| Decree 8.771/2016 | Regulation of the Internet Civil Framework | Research log protection, security and information requests; do not confuse connection records with conversation contents. |
| Consumer Protection Code (CDC), Law 8.078/1990 | Consumer relationships, including concrete B2B assessment | Information, liability, distance contracting and abusive clauses: Articles 6, 14, 25, 39, 46–49, 51 and 54. |
| Civil Code, Law 10.406/2002 | Capacity, contracts, good faith and liability | Select provisions for the contract; do not replace special protective rules with generic clauses. |
| Child and Adolescent Statute (ECA), Law 8.069/1990 | Children and adolescents | Articles 2 and 17 and other relevant provisions; combine with LGPD and current digital legislation. |
| Decree 7.962/2013 | Consumer e-commerce | Identification, offers, service, corrections and withdrawal rights; check current text. |
| Law 15.211/2025 and amendments | Digital products directed at or likely accessed by minors, within legal scope | Consult the Digital ECA and current ANPD regulations; do not rely only on the original ECA. |

Finance, health, education, insurance, telecommunications and public administration may have specific rules. Investigate Bacen/CMN, CVM, Anvisa, ANS or the competent regulator only when evidence indicates applicability. A payment SDK does not automatically make a product a financial institution.

Record territory, role, applicability conditions, provision, wording and source. English language, dollars or foreign servers alone do not determine jurisdiction. Where the EU, UK or California is involved, assess local rules' scope before adding clauses. Use EUR-Lex, EDPB, ICO and official state agencies; identify conflicts and refer for specialist review.
